## 🎯 What This App Does

Generates downloadable QR codes from URLs.
- No external APIs
- No authentication
- Fully responsive
- Dark mode
- Production ready

---

## 💡 First Time?

1. **Run setup**: `setup.sh` or `setup.bat`
2. **Start server**: `php artisan serve`
3. **Open browser**: http://localhost:8000
4. **Generate a QR code**: Enter a URL and click the button
5. **Download**: Click the download button
6. **Explore**: Try dark mode, copy button, etc.
